# Glossary

**Terms, Expressions and Acronyms (TEA) must be organized alphabetically.**


| **_TEA (EN)_**                                                                  | **_TEA (PT)_**                                  | **_Description_** (EN)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |                                       
|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:------------------------------------------------|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Carriage**                                                                    | **Carruagem**                                 | A train component used to transport specific types of cargo or passengers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **City**                                                                        | **Cidade**                                      | Urban area on the map that generates and consumes passengers, mail, and final goods.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **DEP**                                                                         | **DEP**                                   | Acronym for Depot.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Depot**                                                                       | **Depósito**                                 | Smallest type of station, used to supply and collect cargo within a 3x3 radius.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Editor**                                                                      | **Editor**                                      | Person who creates and configures maps and scenarios for simulation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **IND**                                                                         | **IND**                    | Acronym for Industry.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Industry**                                                                    | **Indústria**                | Facility that produces or transforms goods (e.g., coal mine, steel mill).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **LOC**                                                                         | **LOC**                                         | Acronym for Locomotive.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Locomotive**                                                                  | **Locomotiva**                                         | Engine that provides traction to the train, may use steam, diesel, or electricity.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Map Editor**                                                                  | **Editor de Mapas**                                         | Tool used to create and modify the simulation map with static elements.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Passenger**                                                                   | **Passageiro**                  | 	Type of cargo generated and consumed by cities, representing human transport needs.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Player**                                                                      | **Jogador**                                     | User who plays the simulation by managing a railway network.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **PO**                                                                          | **PR**                    | 	Acronym for Product Owner.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Product Owner**                                                               | **Responsável do Produto**                                     | Person representing the organization that defines the product requirements.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Route**                                                                       | **Rota**                     | A list of stations a train will visit to load/unload cargo.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Scenario**                                                                    | **Cenário**                                | A predefined configuration with specific restrictions, historical context, and rules.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Simulator**                                                                   | **Simulador**                                          | Tool used to run and visualize the railway system in operation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **STA**                                                                         | **EST**                            | Acronym for Station.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Station**                                                                     | **Estação**                | Facility allowing interaction between trains and cities or industries. |
| **Terminal**                                                                    | **Terminal**                                 | Largest type of station, covering a 5x5 radius and offering the highest capacity.                                                                                                                       |
| **Track**                                                                       | **Trilho**                                         | Railway connection between two stations, can be single or double, electrified or not.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Train**                                                                       | **Comboio**                                      | A unit composed of a locomotive and carriages used for transporting cargo.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
